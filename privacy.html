Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Vertix Clips is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer and uploads short clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube account, the tool uses Google's sign-in to:

  • upload videos (Shorts) to that channel, and
  • read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of those uploads.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored encrypted in a database on the owner's computer, together with the titles, schedule and statistics of the uploaded Shorts. Nothing is stored on any other server.

What it shares

Nothing. Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is used only to upload and track the owner's own Shorts. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access; disconnecting the account in the tool also deletes the stored tokens.
